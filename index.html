What I'm hired to do

Four areas where a strategy-led PM with design depth moves the needle fastest.

01

Product strategy & discovery

Frame the problem, not just the feature. I run discovery, pressure-test assumptions, and turn messy signals into a roadmap engineering and leadership can commit to. Outcome: fewer wasted quarters, sharper bets.

02

Activation & retention

First use to habit is where most products leak value. I diagnose funnels, prioritise with RICE, run experiments, and ship the changes that move the metric (not the ones that demo well). Neurofenix: +50% activation. Meteor: 0 to 5M, #1 retention in category.

03

Multi-stakeholder platforms in regulated environments

Products where patients, clinicians, executives, and engineers all need different things from the same system, with compliance baked in from day one. Ten years across MedTech, FinTech, and Telecom, including B2B2C pivots and enterprise platform builds.

04

Design-led product calls

I read design at a level most PMs never touch (structure, hierarchy, trust, emotional impact), and I use that depth to sharpen prioritisation, specs, and trade-offs. Not to own Figma, to make the roadmap better.

I specialize in multi-stakeholder platforms in regulated environments, products where patients, clinicians, executives, and engineers all need different things from the same system. That's a product problem most people avoid. I go deep into it.
