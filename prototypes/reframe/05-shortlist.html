Scored for Camille

1
Warm frame

Tortoise, champagne, milky pink, cream. Cool whites and greys clash with warm undertones.

2
Soft cat-eye lift

Or squared cat-eye with subtle upsweep. Lifts the face. Pure rounds and sharp angles don't.

3
Medium, wider than tall

Medium to medium-large, with horizontal extension to balance the face's slight length. Small narrow frames double the length problem.

4
Warm lens tint

Brown, amber, honey gradient make brown eyes glow. Cool blue/pink clashes.

5
Gold at the bridge

Matches existing jewellery and warm palette. Silver on warm frames reads mismatched.
