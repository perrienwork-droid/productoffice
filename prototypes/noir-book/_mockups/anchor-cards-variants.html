Phase C, anchor card variants

Three ways to feature an anchor topic inside a discipline section. Same content (UX section, "Usability" as the anchor), three visual treatments. The plate above each grid is the existing Phase B disc-plate, so what you're judging is just the anchor card behaviour.

Pick one (A, B, or C), or mix elements across variants. Nothing is implemented yet, this file is for the call only.

Variant A
2-wide rectangle, pinned to top of section
Anchor takes 2 of 3 columns in the first row. Same height as a normal card, twice the width. Holds the title, description, and a one-line manifesto blurb. Reads as a section opener that frames the rest.

Variant B
2 x 2 square, pinned to top of section
Anchor takes 2 columns and 2 rows. Strong editorial presence, room for title, description, and three solves bullets. The remaining cards reflow around it. Reads like a magazine feature.

UI Design Patterns

Search, filter, sort interfaces.

Reads well

  • Strongest editorial signal of the three
  • Holds enough content (3 solves) to act as a real entry point
  • Each section gets a clear "headline" before the supporting topics
  • The page stops feeling like a flat grid immediately

Trade-offs

  • Disrupts the row count math, the 2.5-row preview from B.4 needs to count an anchor as 2 rows
  • Mobile fallback, can only stack to full width
  • Most content of the three, needs solves data (already in manifest)
  • Four sections of B.B.B.B. could feel repetitive on a long scroll
Variant C
Natural sort position, accent chrome (no grid span)
Anchor stays in its natural position in the sort order. Same footprint as a normal card, but with white background, full discipline-color border, and a 3px left accent. Subtle, in-the-flow, doesn't disturb layout.
